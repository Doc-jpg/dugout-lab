# Design principles

Deren, 2026-11-02: "blend comprehensiveness, thoroughness and granularity with ease of use, quick interactions, and a clean, simple UI for actual users who aren't technical at all." These are the rules every screen is held to.

1. **Two taps during practice.** Anything a coach does with a ball in the air is name, then one button. The four common reads are big buttons; the rest sit behind "More". Free text is there but never required.
2. **One screen before practice.** Quick start generates from the usual setup in one tap. The full form is on the same screen underneath for the days that are different.
3. **Depth behind a fold, never gone.** Profiles, drills and plans hold everything the master prompt asked for; the first view shows the basics and a heading opens the rest. Nothing is removed to look simple.
4. **Words a parent uses.** No codes in the interface (no "L1-5", no "DNB" without the words next to it). Style names read as "reps", "mixed reps", "make a read", "competition".
5. **Nothing is required to start.** A team name and a roster get a coach to a generated practice. Goals, levels, parents, logo and colors can be added when they come up, from the place they are used.
6. **Defaults do the work.** Usual length, coaches, gear and space come from Settings; the plan comes from goals and notes; drafts come from the recap. The coach edits, he does not assemble.
7. **Say why.** Every objective shows where it came from; every plan has "Why this plan"; every suggestion lists what it rests on.
8. **Granular when the coach wants it.** Per-player versions of a drill, per-area levels, per-note categories and drill tags are all there for the coach who wants them, and invisible to the one who does not.
9. **Every surface works offline and one-handed on a phone.** Big targets, bottom sheets, no hover-only controls, timer readable in sunlight.
10. **Test the path a new coach walks.** The acceptance script starts from an empty phone and goes through setup, plan, live, recap, family, backup and offline. Anything a non-technical coach must do is in that script.
